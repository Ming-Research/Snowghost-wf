# X1, X3 and X16: how local real edits are, which offset structure, and how much of an edit lies near the viewport

Run 2026-10-03 on Snowghost main fcb80cb (worktree branch exp/x1, no code
changed), 4-vCPU Xeon container, every build and timed run under the host
lock. The full tables are `agg-apollo11.md`, `agg-html5.md` and
`agg-ecma262.md`, generated by `agg.py` from the per-edit results (197, 183
and 77 edits, all valid; the raw per-edit JSON, 170 MB, is not kept).
Scripts ran with the run host's paths.

## Verdicts

| Exp | Criterion (written before the run) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| X1 | context plus paragraph is the layout unit if fewer than 1 percent of paragraphs change lines for word edits | **Pass on all three pages.** A word edit changes 1 paragraph in 68 of 70 edits and 2 in the other 2: at most 0.17 % of paragraphs on apollo11 (30 edits), 0.003 % on html5 (30) and 0.006 % on ecma262 (10). |
| X1 | recomputed set over minimal set under 3 | **Holds for paragraphs and contexts; fails for styled nodes on root-level edits.** Paragraphs are under 3 in 95 to 98.5 % of edits (median 1.00). Contexts are 1.00 everywhere. Styled nodes, with the restyle subtree as the recomputed set, exceed 3 for 13 % of apollo11's class, colour and font-size edits (2 to 4 % elsewhere); root custom-property and `html` font-size edits give 10^2 to 10^5. |
| X3 | adopt the tree or prefix sums where the context-relative scheme's p90 rewrite count exceeds theirs by more than the per-write cost ratio | **Pass on all three pages, html5 included.** p90(context-relative) / p90(tree) is 5.1 on apollo11, 509 on html5 and 819 on ecma262, against a measured per-write cost ratio of 0.5 to 2.1. |
| X16 | viewport-first pays if, for edits costing more than one frame, the median share of dirty units within one viewport height of the viewport is under one half | **Pass, on 13 edits.** The median share is 0.017 within 720 px of the edit point and 0.024 for the viewport plus 720 px each side. The edits that cost more than a frame are whole-document edits; edits under a frame have all their work at the edit point. |

## Method

**Edits.** Scripted, seeded source edits on copies of the pages:
- word (inserted mid-sentence in a text span of at least 90 characters), sentence, class (a class named by a page rule, added to a random box), colour (inline), block (a `<p>` inserted before a random `<p>`), fontsize (130 % on a container with at least 5 descendants), rootfont (`html` at 12, 20 and 24 px) and custom (a `var()`-used property on `:root` or inline on `html`);
- apollo11: 30 per kind (197 edits, 14 custom); html5: 30 per kind (183; the page uses no `var()`); ecma262: a sample of 10 per kind, 14 custom, 3 rootfont (77 edits), since a dump takes 9 s there.

**Diff.** Full layout and style dumps before and after each edit, compared by
value and aligned by shifted preorder index, (parent, ordinal) for text nodes
and (context root, run) for paragraphs. Element names were checked at every
index.

**Checks.**
- The 197 apollo11 edits regenerated from the seed match the stopped first round's results in every old field.
- Diffing a page against itself gives 0 for every set and count.
- A defect was found and fixed: html5 and ecma262 are 1.15 M and 1.28 M px tall, and the dump's absolute y is float32, so past 2^19 its spacing exceeds a 0.1 px tolerance. On html5 that produced 1,014 phantom moved boxes per edit (differences of exactly 0.125 px). The tolerance is now max(0.1, 4 ulp(page height)): 0.5 px on html5 and ecma262.

**Not checked.** That the contexts and paragraphs rebuilt from the dump equal
the stage's. Contexts are close (apollo11 1,136 against the stage's 1,113,
html5 16,066 against 13,843, ecma262 11,223 against 10,217); dump paragraphs
are about half the stage's (1,198 against 2,569; 32,684 against 60,868;
35,504 against 57,514), so paragraph percentages are overstated by up to 2.1
times.

**X3 write counts**, for edits that change a box's height or insert a block:
(a) absolute positions; (b) offsets relative to the parent box; (c) offsets
relative to the formatting-context root (Q55); (d) offsets anchored to the
previous sibling's end; (e) a summary tree, ceil(log2 siblings) node writes
per changed child; (f) prefix sums, one write per in-flow box whose own size
changed. The per-write cost ratio comes from `wcost.c` (gcc -O2, hot cache):
a context-relative rewrite (read-modify-write of one 64-byte record in a
streaming loop) costs 0.55 to 2.5 ns, a tree write (one parent from its two
children) 1.1 to 1.3 ns; tree over flat is 2.1 at N = 1,024, 0.8 at 4,096,
0.9 at 20,919 and 0.5 at 117,137.

**X16.**
- *Window.* A 1280×720 viewport containing the edit point, the top of the edited paragraph or element. Root edits have no point, so they average over viewports at 10, 30, 50, 70 and 90 % of the page height. "Within one viewport height of the edit point" is ±720 px; the table's wording, within one viewport height of the viewport, is ±1080 px. Both are reported.
- *Near.* A dirty unit is near if its vertical extent after the edit meets the window. Units: styled nodes, paragraphs, contexts, and boxes whose size or position changed.
- *Cost.* styled nodes × t_style + paragraphs × t_text + contexts × t_layout, with the full stages' per-unit sequential times, (T(reps) − T(0))/reps, best of 3 (`timing.py`, `results/timing.json`). Boxes that only move cost 0, since offsets translate them. One frame is 16.7 ms.

## X3 results

Qualifying edits: apollo11 112 of 197, html5 97 of 183, ecma262 32 of 77.
Rewrites at p90:

| page | a absolute | b parent-relative | c context-relative | d sibling | e tree | f prefix sums | c/e | c/f | c/d |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| apollo11 | 16,489 | 42 | 328 | 17 | 64 | 45 | 5.1 | 7.3 | 19 |
| html5 | 213,032 | 2,956 | 24,213 | 6.4 | 48 | 11 | 509 | 2,124 | 3,783 |
| ecma262 | 348,445 | 51 | 33,006 | 3 | 40 | 21 | 819 | 1,602 | 11,002 |

- Median rewrites for c: 187, 12,445 and 19,118. Absolute positions move 13,000, 116,000 and 193,000 boxes at the median.
- By kind (c/e at p90): word edits qualify only 6, 2 and 1 times and give 6.6, 1,406 and 1,523; sentence edits (26, 16, 8) give 8.5, 645 and 1,050; block edits (30, 30, 10) give 10.8, 851 and 1,199.
- d, e and f are all far below c, but d needs a walk to read a position (html5's body has 3,545 children). The write counts do not separate the tree from prefix sums (a Fenwick prefix sum is the same tree); that needs the read side, not measured.
- Parent-relative offsets (b), outside the criterion, have p90 42 and 51 on apollo11 and ecma262, below c, because c also rewrites every block of the context below the edit across nesting levels. b loses only on flat parents (html5: 2,956).

## X16 results

**Cost per edit.** Local kinds cost far less than a frame (median word 0.05
to 0.07 ms, sentence 0.1 to 0.2, class 0.05 to 0.15, block 0.07 to 0.2). 13 of
457 edits cost more than one frame:
- apollo11, 9: rootfont ×3 at 1,058 ms each; custom 954, 485 and 89 ms; fontsize 53 and 31 ms; one container colour edit 40 ms;
- html5, 3: rootfont, 3,099 ms each;
- ecma262, 1: a custom property, 63 ms.

Over 4 ideal workers, 9 of the 13 remain over a frame.

**Median share of dirty units near the edit:**

| edits | n | in viewport (±360) | within 720 | viewport ±720 (±1080) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| over one frame, all pages, pooled | 13 | 0.010 | 0.017 | 0.024 |
| same, styled nodes, paragraphs, contexts only | 13 | 0.011 | 0.017 | 0.023 |
| same, cost-weighted | 13 | 0.010 | 0.016 | 0.022 |
| apollo11 | 9 | 0.014 | 0.023 | 0.032 |
| html5 | 3 | 0.001 | 0.001 | 0.001 |
| ecma262 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| under one frame, styled nodes, paragraphs, contexts | 444 | | 1.00 | |
| under one frame, with moved boxes | 444 | | 0.029 | |

- The largest shares among the 13: an apollo11 container colour edit 0.63; a container font-size edit 0.32 pooled, 0.89 without moved boxes.
- Threshold sensitivity (median within 720, pooled / work units only): 16.7 ms, 13 edits, 0.017 / 0.017; 4.2 ms, 28 edits, 0.022 / 0.035; 1 ms, 58 edits, 0.016 / 0.94. Between 1 and 4 ms the edits are local to a container and their work sits next to the edit.
- An alternative model charging every moved box the mean box cost puts 91, 95 and 32 edits over a frame; their pooled share within 720 is 0.013, 0.001 and 0.001, but the work-unit share is 0.86 to 1.00. It was dropped because translated boxes cost nothing with relative offsets.
- Time to a correct viewport (cost × cost-weighted near share): apollo11 rootfont 15 to 67 ms of 1,058 ms; apollo11 custom 21 and 19 ms of 954 and 485 ms; html5 rootfont 3 to 7 ms of 3,099 ms.
- For ordinary edits, more than 98 % of boxes that only move lie outside the window; a sentence edit moves 7,400, 19,700 and 64,000 boxes, a block insertion 7,500, 67,600 and 62,200.

## Limits

- Single, synthetic edits on loaded pages, uniform over text spans of at least 90 characters (favouring long paragraphs) and over boxes. No script, hover, parser appends or font arrival.
- Minimal sets come from output diffs, not from an incremental implementation; recomputed sets are modelled, not run. Text is compared by fragment count and width, not break position.
- ecma262 has 10 edits per kind; html5 has no custom-property edits.
- The 0.5 px tolerance on html5 and ecma262 hides smaller real moves.
- X3 counts writes, not reads; the cost ratio is an in-cache C micro-benchmark; no tree was implemented.
- X16's per-unit times are whole-stage averages; a dirty context is charged the mean context time; the edit point is the top of the edited element; the parallel speedup is an ideal 4.
- The sequential style stage measured here is 0.98 s on apollo11 (83 µs per node, cause not investigated), 1.85 s on html5 and 2.37 s on ecma262.
