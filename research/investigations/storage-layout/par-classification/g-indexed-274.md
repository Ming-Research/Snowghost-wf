## Helper narrowing and operator forms

No helper effect or signature change alone can admit any of the currently denied complete loops. In particular, `set_coverage_bits` and `mark_lookups` already take their mutable primitive-array roots separately from the read-only layout table. Their stores use a previously computed temporary rather than an operation on the target cell in the `set`. Their callers retain whole-root write effects; the release inspects the written checked loop body, not an inlined helper implementation. The table also identifies cases where narrowing an independent-output helper would still leave an unsupported shared record reduction.

The required forms are concrete: coverage/lookup bits need `set flags[e] = ior(flags[e], bit);`; row maxima need `set tops[e] = imax(tops[e], ascent);`; a histogram needs `set counts[e] = counts[e] +wrap 1_u64;`. A Boolean sticky mark needs `set flags[e] = bor(flags[e], yes);` with `yes = True()`, rather than `set flags[e] = True();`. Integer one-marks need `ior(cell, 1_u8)` and clearing zero-marks needs `iand(cell, 0_u8)`. These are operation-form illustrations, not complete programs or compiled predictions. Other occurrences and all other loop conditions must still pass.

**Applied source diffs: none.** Replacing direct stores, folding temporary calculations into stores, hoisting a root length out of a direct loop, changing `+sat` or exact `+` to `+wrap`, or replacing record/winner representations goes beyond effect/signature-only helper narrowing. No such change was made or counted. No after-change report was needed because the renderer tree, pin and report command did not change.

The language and implementation grounds for this distinction are [PAR-2](https://github.com/Ming-Research/Whitefoot/blob/691ea81069202c88f17df9ce0005d17a6d47ada7/spec/kernel-spec.md), and the release's [`indexed_combine` / `indexed_state` / `record_writes`](https://github.com/Ming-Research/Whitefoot/blob/691ea81069202c88f17df9ce0005d17a6d47ada7/compiler/src/semantic/loop_permission.rs): the RHS must be the admitted operation, non-update root occurrences are rejected, and a call's projected writes are not an indexed reduction update. This source inspection explains the observed denials; the counted verdicts themselves come only from the hosted ledger.

## Findings for Whitefoot and limits

- The diagnostic “a constant mark is not an update” also covers nonconstant temporary RHS values, such as `marked`, `combined`, `larger` and `next`. The full per-site diagnostics above preserve what the release actually said; they do not imply that those source computations are constants. No diagnostic was changed here.
- Even the directly written reach histogram (`style/restyle.wf:97`) is denied under condition 1: its bounds guard reads `starts.inner.len` in the loop body, outside the cell update. Measure-read permission for element families does not make this indexed-root occurrence pass. This is an observed limitation of the release, not a request to weaken bounds checking or a measured admission after hoisting.
- Constant marks, record-cell reductions, hidden-border winners, key-dependent cascade payloads, and validation/error exits remain separate limitations. A first-condition report does not exhaust all blockers; the table's helper assessment names additional visible blockers where needed.
- The earlier forecast of 16–22 whole loops with local helper narrowing is not achieved by this release on the hand-written-page source under the stated rewrite limit. This experiment does not measure what broader canonical-store rewrites or later compiler changes would permit, nor does it measure runtime speed or establish dynamic hotness.

Disposition: the measurement and diagnostic limitations are reported here for Whitefoot; no compiler issue, renderer workaround, design change, TODO edit or pull request was created. The pin moved only as requested, and no Whitefoot gap was separately filed. The temporary workflow remains reproducible on this work branch and is to be removed when the investigation is closed.
