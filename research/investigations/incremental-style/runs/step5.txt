Summary:

- Sequentially, Snowghost leads on 15 of 18 page and kind pairs. It
  trails on ecma262's font size (1.8x to 2.1x) and on the three block edits.
- Block edits still build a whole new style state. They belong to M2
  (research/investigations/structure-edits).
- At four workers it trails on apollo11's and html5's colour edits, by 9
  and 3 us. The runtime's fixed cost on sub-millisecond work (a Whitefoot
  requirement in docs/todo.md) exceeds the work: the sequential restyle is
  20 us.
- html5's colour seq medians spread from 19 to 34 us across runs; the
  others spread under 20 percent.

M1's criterion 2:

- Colour and font size are within 2x Chromium sequentially on all three
  pages. ecma262's font size reaches 1.8 to 2.1x; its 924 us run is 2.12x.
- Root font size is at or below Chromium at four workers on all three
  pages.

Criterion 3, locality:

- A colour edit visits 2 to 14 elements in style, the edited element and
  its children (runs/step3.txt).
- Layout reports prepared 0, contexts 0 and paragraphs 0 for every edit.
- Not separately shown: toggling a class that no rule names restyles
  nothing.

Criterion 4, full build:

- layout_oracle layout 3 against main 29f89af, built with the same compiler
  (seconds, two runs each):
  - html5: 4.16 and 3.73 against 2.62 and 2.61;
  - ecma262: 3.62 and 3.59 against 2.93 and 2.94;
  - apollo11: 0.87 and 0.87 against 0.33 and 0.33.
- So the full build is faster than main on every page.
