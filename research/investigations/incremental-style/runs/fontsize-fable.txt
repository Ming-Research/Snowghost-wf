Question and criterion (written before measuring)
------------------------------------------------
Where do the roughly 1.5 ms (median) of a sfontsize edit on ecma262 go,
given that the reported update counts (contexts 3, entries 2) describe
almost no work? The supplied timing lines already split the interval
into delta_us (marking), picks_us and the remainder (update); the sample
of a long-running script gives flat stacks (no frame pointers), so the
attribution instrument is the update's own counts, propagated through
the paths that discard them today (lay_out_child, stack_flow's tallies,
the container path of update_held, positioned children).

Comparison: the base driver built from the unchanged f9c5bfe source
against a driver that only propagates counts (no layout change), on the
same ten sfontsize edits; then against the implementation. Byte identity
of every incremental dump with a full rebuild is required for every
script listed in the task. Timing is the median of the ten edits' us.

Rejection: the count-propagation diagnosis is rejected if the propagated
counts of the slow edits (7-10, about 10 ms each) stay near those of the
fast edits; then the cost is in a pass the counts do not describe and a
timing-only variant must isolate it. An implementation is rejected if
any identity check reports DIFF or refused, if ecma262's median does not
fall, or if html5, apollo11 or another edit kind becomes slower in a
same-source paired comparison.
