Reading
  Text edits: Snowghost is ahead on html5 (0.02 to 0.08 of Chromium) and
  ecma262 (0.28 to 0.91), and level with or behind Chromium on apollo11
  at four workers (word 269 against 200 us, 1.34; sentence 0.70) and level
  sequentially (0.94, 0.50). By the criterion written before the run, the
  proposal is rejected on apollo11's word edits at four workers. Chromium's
  text edits on ecma262 and apollo11 take 2 to 4 ticks of its 100 us timer,
  so those ratios carry up to 50 percent of timer error; the next pass
  takes Chromium's durations from its trace events.
  Class and block edits: Snowghost is 150 to 7,000 times slower, almost
  all of it the style stage run in full; its layout part alone is below
  Chromium's whole cost only for html5's fontsize and colour edits. A root
  font change, which restyles and lays out nearly everything in both, is
  1.2 (html5) to 3.7 (apollo11) times Chromium's at four workers.
