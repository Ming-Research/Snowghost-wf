E1: one edit's cost in Chromium and in Snowghost, per page and edit kind
(DESIGN.md in this directory). Every command ran from the repository root,
under the host lock.

Environment
  Apple M1 Pro, 8 cores, 32 GB, macOS 26.6.2. Chromium 141.0.7390.37
  (Playwright build 1194), headless, 1280 by 720, scripts disabled, the page
  served as http://localhost/page/index.html with COOP and COEP so that it
  is cross-origin isolated and performance.now resolves 5 us; every served
  stylesheet checked loaded (a 404 variant fails the check). Snowghost:
  research/x5-timing-fix's oracle with style_us, drivers seq 2def040b...,
  par f9c4e430... (--par, WF_WORKERS=4), pinned compiler 3b1ff1c4...;
  inputs, fonts and edit scripts of incremental-layout/runs/step4b.txt.

Runs
  Chromium: chromium.mjs on every page and kind, twice; per edit the faster
  of the two, then the median. The two runs' medians agree within 15
  percent except ecma262 fontsize (445 and 785 us).
  Snowghost: run.sh time par and seq, RUNS 1 (build/e1/session.out).
  A first Chromium pass with performance.now at its default 100 us
  resolution gave the same picture; one with the disabled-by-default
  devtools.timeline trace categories made Chromium itself several times
  slower (ecma262 word edits 3.0 ms against 0.3 ms) and is not used.

Reading
  Text edits: Snowghost is 11 to 35 times faster on html5; on ecma262 and
  apollo11 it is between 0.35 and 1.44 of Chromium at four workers and
  between 0.28 and 0.94 sequentially, so the four-worker runtime's cost
  on edits of a few hundred microseconds decides those two pages. By the
  criterion written before the run, the proposal is rejected at four
  workers on ecma262's sentence edits (1.44) and apollo11's word edits
  (1.34); it holds sequentially on every page.
  Class and block edits: Snowghost is 150 to 23,000 times slower. The full
  style stage is most of it, but the layout part alone is also 11 to 1,600
  times Chromium's whole cost: the style delta and the marking walk visit
  every element and context on every style edit, where Chromium's colour
  edit costs 25 to 35 us.
  A root font change, which redoes nearly everything in both: Snowghost's
  layout part is 0.34 to 0.73 of Chromium's whole cost, the full style
  stage makes the whole 1.2 to 3.7 times.
