Soft wrap opportunities at the start of a bidi-isolating inline box (Chromium, headless)

Question: whether a line may break before an inline box whose unicode-bidi is not normal. The
citation marks of MediaWiki pages are `sup.reference { unicode-bidi: isolate; white-space:
nowrap }`, and an infobox cell's min-content width, and with it the split of the table's
columns, depends on it.

Probe (build/probes/bd1.html, a `width:min-content` table of `border-spacing:0` with one cell
of 14px sans-serif, widths of the cell):

  content of the cell                                       Chromium   without the rule
  aaaa bbb.<span unicode-bidi:isolate>[5]</span> cc dd       42.83      31.16
  aaaa bbb.<span>[5]</span> cc dd                            31.16      31.16
  aaaa bbb.<span unicode-bidi:isolate-override>...           42.83      31.16
  ... embed, bidi-override, plaintext, <bdi>, <span dir=ltr> 42.83      31.16
  aaaa bbb<span isolate>cc dd</span> ee ff                   37.36      37.36
  aaaa bbb<span isolate>cc</span>ee ff                       52.94      52.94
  aaaa bbb.<span isolate>(5)</span>ee ff                     59.95      32.69

"bbb.[5]" stays one word when the box opens with a bidi control, "bbb.(5)ee" too; a break
after the box (before " cc") and inside it is unaffected. Blink writes the control
(U+2068 and the like) into the text, and the line breaker finds no opportunity before it.

A break after a space stays (probe of 14px monospace, `aa <span isolate>bbbbbbbbbb</span>`
84.30 in Chromium, 109.58 when the opportunity is dropped too; `aa<span isolate> bbbbbbbbbb</span>`
and the NBSP case unchanged), as the line breaker treats the control as a letter after a space
and as part of the previous scalar after anything else (UAX 14, LB9 and LB10).

Rule: no soft wrap opportunity before the index where an inline box whose unicode-bidi is not
normal opens, unless a space (U+0020) precedes it (a mandatory break stays). Chromium's user agent sheet gives unicode-bidi: isolate to
every block-level element of its list (address, article, ..., li, ul, td, ...), to bdi, output and
every element with a dir attribute of ltr, rtl or auto, isolate-override to bdo and plaintext to
pre and textarea with dir=auto (computed on `<tag>` and `<tag dir=...>` for the tags of the HTML
standard; renderer/style/ua.css lists them).

Consequence on apollo11: the infobox cell "109,646 lb (49,735 kg)[5]" has min-content 85.78 in
Chromium, 73.22 without the rule; inserting U+2060 before each `sup.reference` in the page (an
equivalent break opportunity filter) takes the non-multicolumn block-level boxes from 78.7 to
85.7 percent.
