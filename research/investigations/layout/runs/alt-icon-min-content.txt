Min-content width of an img's alt text box (Chromium, headless)

Probe (build/probes/alt2.html, 14.08px sans-serif, `border-spacing:0`, cells without padding):

  content                                                         Chromium  icon inline (ours before)
  td width:1px > a white-space:nowrap > img alt="Emblem of the Apollo program"   189.375   205.375
  table width:min-content > td > img alt="Emblem of the Apollo" white-space:nowrap  133.047   149.047
  the same, width:max-content                                     149.047   149.047
  the same, width:min-content, wrapping alt text                  51.641    51.641

The box of an img showing its alt text holds the 16 px broken-image icon, which floats, and the
text. A float sits beside the lines, so for the min-content width it is a run of its own: the
width is the larger of the icon and the line's min-content width, not their sum, while the
max-content width is the sum (149.047 = 16 + 133.047). apollo11's navbox emblem is such a box in
a width:1px cell; the cell was 16 px too wide and the table's columns, and the rows beside
them, followed.
