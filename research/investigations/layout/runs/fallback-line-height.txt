Line height with a fallback face (Chromium, headless, 1280x720, fonts of build/fonts)

Question: how a glyph drawn by a face other than the primary one changes the height of its
line when line-height is normal.

Probe: a div of white-space nowrap with `font: SIZEpx 'Liberation Sans'` holding "Hxg" alone,
the same with "Hxg" followed by an Ethiopic word drawn by FreeSerif (CSS.getPlatformFontsForNode
reports FreeSerif for it), and a div with `font: SIZEpx FreeSerif` holding "Hxg"; the divs'
heights, one probe per SIZE:

  size  Liberation Sans  Liberation Sans + Ethiopic  FreeSerif
  14    16               17                          15
  20    23               23                          22
  40    45               46                          44
  60    69               70                          66

Face metrics (rounded each on its own, as face_extents gives them): Liberation Sans ascent,
descent, gap 13,3,0 / 18,4,1 / 36,8,1 / 54,13,2; FreeSerif (USE_TYPO_METRICS set, so its OS/2
typographic metrics) 11,3,1 / 16,4,2 / 32,8,4 / 48,12,6.

Rule that fits all four sizes: the line-height box of a normal line height is the ascent plus
the top half of the line gap above the baseline and the descent plus the rest below it (the
top half rounded down); the line takes the largest ascent and descent over the primary face
and every fallback face that draws a glyph of the box. 14: (13,3) and (11,4) give 13+4=17;
20: (18,5) and (17,5) give 23; 40: (36,9) and (34,10) give 46; 60: (55,14) and (51,15) give 70.
Adding the fallback's bare ascent and descent without its gap (11,3 at 14) would give 16, not 17.

With a numeric or length line-height the fallback face changes nothing (the box's line-height
box is fixed). A fallback face whose glyphs come from an embedded bitmap strike (WenQuanYi Zen Hei
at 14 px, line 18) takes its metrics from the strike, which face_extents does not reproduce.
