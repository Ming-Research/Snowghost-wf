Chromium probe: the intrinsic widths of a block holding floats (width: min-content / max-content on the block;
body font 16px/20px serif, float 30px wide, "aaa bbb" 49.3125, "aaa" 21.3125, "bbb" 24).
min-content: the widest float or word, floats do not add:
  float 30 + "float": 30      float 30 + float 20 + "float": 30      float 30 + "aaa bbbbbbbb ccc": 64
max-content: the widths of the floats in a line chunk add to the widest line beside them; a block boundary ends the chunk:
  "aaa bbb"                                  49.3125
  float, "aaa bbb"                           79.3125
  "aaa bbb", float (after the text)          79.3125
  "aaa", float, " bbb"                       79.3125
  float, "aaa", br, "bbb"                    51.3125   (the float joins the line it sits in)
  "aaa", br, float, "bbb"                    54        (float 30 + "bbb" 24)
  float 30, float right 20, "aaa bbb"        99.3125
  float, p("aaa bbb")                        49.3125   (a block child is its own chunk, the float alone is 30)
  p, float, p                                49.3125
  float alone 30; float 30 + float 20 alone  50
  float, div{clear:both}("aaa bbb")          49.3125
  float, "aaa", div{clear:both}, "bbb"       51.3125
apollo11 and the table case in tests/layout/table-cases.html (a cell with a float and a word) take their column from this max-content.

(the float joins only the line it sits in); the case is left out of tests/layout/flow-cases.html.
