A line whose first run does not fit beside a float (Chromium, headless)

Probe (build/probes/fl1.html, 14px sans-serif, a 400 px box holding a float:right of height 30 px
and then a paragraph "In spite of that the proposed program faced opposition."):

  float                                  room beside it   Chromium: first text line
  width 380px, margin-left 30px (outer 410)  none (x = -10)  y = 30 (below the float)
  width 380px, margin-left 10px (outer 390)  10 px            y = 30
  width 390px (outer 390)                    10 px            y = 30

Without the float the text is one line of 339.34 px. "In" is 13.3 px wide, so the room of 10 px
holds no content: the line moves down to the float's bottom (CSS 2.2 9.5, "shifted downward until
either some content fits or there are no more floats"), where the whole text fits on one line. The
same page with 25 px of room (width 375px) puts "In" beside the float and the rest below
(tests/layout/flow-cases.html, fourth section from the end). apollo11's wide thumbnail figure
(margin-left 24 px, wider than its box) leaves a 22 px gap, and Chromium starts the paragraph
below it, where ours put one word on each of four lines beside it.

Rule: when a line's first unbreakable run is wider than the room the floats leave beside the
line (the room is narrower than the box), the line moves down to the next float bottom and is
tried again; a line that would be wider than the room in an empty box stays where it is.
