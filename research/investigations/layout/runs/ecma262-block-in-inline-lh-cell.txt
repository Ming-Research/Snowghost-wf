ecma262 layout classes (Chromium, headless; tests/layout/flow-cases.html and table-cases.html, first sections)

1. Where an inline element's empty rectangle before a block in it sits. ecmarkup's emu-table, emu-grammar
   and emu-alg are unknown elements, so display:inline around block-level figures, productions and lists.
   Probe: `<div style="height:100px">x</div><span><figure style="margin:10px 0">fig</figure></span>`.
   The span's first rectangle (0 x 0) is at y = 112, the figure's border-box top, not at 102 where the
   margin begins. Variants (the figure's margin collapsing with the sibling's before it, 18px note then
   27px figure: 172 = 145 + 27):
     after a closed block or a child context (a flex box)            at the block's top, after the margins
     first in a block with padding-top:1px, margin 10px              at the padding edge, above the margin
     after a paragraph with lines (text, then the span)              at the line top, above the margin
     after a block that is itself inside an inline element (`<g><div>`), then the span
                                                                     at the line after it, above the margin
   The last case is ecma262's `emu-grammar` (inline, a block production in it) followed by `emu-alg`.

2. The lh unit (CSS Values 4). The ecma262 page's own style sheet gives the table corner cell `height: 2lh` (line-height 27px, so a
   54px content box; Chromium's th is 69.375 px with 7.2px paddings and half a collapsed border), and its
   `.slash` and `.column`/`.row` children are absolutely positioned against it.

3. An absolutely positioned child of a table cell. The cell is position:relative; the child's
   `width:100%; height:100%` is the cell's padding box after the row has been sized: 253.375 x 68.375 at
   (0.5, 0.5) in a 254.375 x 69.375 cell with half of the collapsed 1px border (the border the table uses,
   not the style's).

4. A line-height of 1em on a 19.98px font is 19.984375 px (rounded to the nearest unit), not 19.96875;
   the 0.03125 px per heading added up to a 1 px drift over 35 sections (see line-height-rounding.txt).

Scores, ecma262 blocks: 98.69% before, 99.82% after (40112 of 40182 boxes). What remains is a handful of
lines that wrap differently by a sub-pixel width (an emu-nt that ends 0.01 px inside the line in one
renderer and 0.03 px beyond it in the other, 83416): each such line moves every box below it by a line
height, so one wrap is about ten ancestor emu-clause heights.
