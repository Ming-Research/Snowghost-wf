Chromium observations from the line-fill case page (tests/layout/line-fill-cases.html), line-fill-temp CI run 38236021036 at cbe385b, Playwright 1.56.1 Chromium on ubuntu-24.04, serif resolving to a Times-metric face (space 0.25em).

1. Which font measures a tab inside an inline box with another font.
Case: <div style="white-space: pre">x<span style="font-size: 32px">TAB</span>y</div>, body font 16px serif, tab-size 8.
  piece        x (from the line start)   width
  "x"          0                          8
  span / TAB   8                          24
  "y"          32                         8
The tab ends at 32 px, the first multiple of 8 x 4 px (the block's 16px space) past 8. Measured by the span's own 32px font (space 8 px, stops every 64 px), it would end at 64 px and be 56 px wide.
So Chromium measures a tab by its block container's font, not the inline box's. CSS Text 3 4.2 measures tab-size in "the space character's advance width of the nearest block container ancestor", which matches. Where tab-size itself comes from (the block or the inline box) is not observed here: the case sets tab-size on the block only.

2. Preserved spaces that hang at a soft wrap (white-space: pre-wrap, div 200px wide).
Case: "Hanging spaces" then 30 spaces then words.
  line 1 fragments: text 0..100.86, spaces 100.86..220.86 (30 x 4 px = 120 px, ending 20.86 px past the line's end)
Chromium's getClientRects reports the full width of hanging spaces; it does not clamp them to the line's end. CSS Text 3 4.1.3 lets a UA visually collapse hanging spaces; Chromium does not do so here.

3. Preserved spaces before a forced break (white-space: pre-wrap, div 200px wide).
Case: "Before a forced break" then 20 spaces then a newline, then "the spaces stay."
  line 1 fragments: text 0..139.03, spaces 139.03..219.03 (80 px, past the 200 px line end), newline 219.03 (0 wide)
  line 2: "the spaces stay."
The spaces hang: they neither push the line to wrap nor move the newline to a line of its own, and they form a fragment of their own between the text and the newline.
Counter-case (tests/layout/flow-cases.html, run 38240329524 at 6bf3434): pre-wrap lines "two lines" and "trailing spaces before a break", each followed by two spaces and a newline on a line 1254 px wide, where the spaces fit. Chromium keeps those spaces in the text's fragment (e.g. "two lines  " one fragment 65.34 px, then the newline). So the spaces take a fragment of their own only when the line overflows with them. CSS Text 3 4.1.3 has spaces before a forced break "conditionally hang" (hang only where they do not fit), which Chromium's two-line result matches.

4. A tab is an item of its own (same run 38236021036; confirmed again in run 38240329524 at 6bf3434, where every line-fill case matched).
Every tab run in white-space: pre text is a text fragment of its own in getClientRects: "a<TAB>bc<TAB>def<TAB>g" in monospace gives 7 fragments on one line (a, tab, bc, tab, def, tab, g), and two tabs in a row give one fragment.

5. text-align: justify (div 200px wide, run 38240329524 at 6bf3434, where all five justify paragraphs matched Snowghost within 1px).
  "Justified text spreads the free space ..." lines 1-3 are 200 px wide, the last line 160.84 px (its natural width).
  "A forced<br>break ends ..." line 1 "A forced" is 55.53 px (natural: a forced break ends it), the next three lines are 200 px, the last 27.11 px.
  "Inline <b>boxes</b> and <span class=pad>padded spans</span> ..." measured from the line's start, the b starts at 53.03 px and is 38.23 px wide (its own text gets no share: it holds no space), and the span starts at 145.80 px and wraps to the next line.
  "Averyveryverylongwordwithnospaces fits nowhere." the overflowing first line has no opportunity and stays at its natural width.
  "An indented justified paragraph ..." with text-indent 20px: lines a soft wrap ends fill the 200 px from the indent.
Snowghost's model, an equal share of the line's free space to each space, tab and no-break space before the line's last scalar, the last line and a line a forced break ends left at the start, places every inline box after each space where Chromium does; a share that grew with the opportunity's own width, or that went to the gap after the last word, would move the b and the span. The tab and no-break space as opportunities are not observed yet (predictions P9 and P10 below).

Predictions for the cases added after run 38240329524 (the last section of tests/layout/line-fill-cases.html, plus a third section of justified paragraphs), written before CI ran them. Serif is a Times-metric face: a 7.11 px, b 8, c 7.11, d 8, e 7.11, f 5.33, g 8, x 8, space 4; tab-size 8 gives stops every 32 px.
P1. <div class="pre-wrap" style="width: min-content">ab TAB x</div>. Chromium treats the space and the tab as one run of white space, breaks after the run, and measures the tab where it falls (after "ab ", at 19.11 px, so it reaches 32), counting it: min-content 32 px. If the tab hangs at the line's end as the space does, 16 px. Prediction 32 px, sure 3/5. Snowghost gives 32 px either way (it breaks between the space and the tab and measures the tab alone from 0, also 32).
P2. Same with "abcdefg TAB x". Measured in place the tab runs from 54.66 to 64: min-content 64 px. Measured alone from 0 after a break between the space and the tab (Snowghost now): the widest run is "abcdefg", 50.66 px, so 51. If the tab hangs: 51 as well. Prediction 64 px, sure 2/5; if it holds, Snowghost must stop breaking between a preserved space and a following tab, and the intrinsic walk must measure the tab in place.
P3. <div class="pre" style="width: max-content">ab TAB cd TAB x</div>: 15.11, tab to 32, cd to 47.11, tab to 64, x to 72: max-content 72 px. Sure 4/5.
P4. <div class="pre-wrap" style="width: max-content">abc + 5 spaces</div>: the spaces at the paragraph's end hang unconditionally (CSS Text 3 4.1.3) and add nothing to max-content: width 22.22 px, the text "abc" one fragment and the 20 px of spaces a second fragment running past the box. Sure 2/5: Chromium may count them (width 42.22, one fragment).
P5. letter-spacing 2px on "a TAB b" in pre: the tab interval stays 8 bare spaces, 32 px; "a" is 9.11 px with its spacing, the tab 22.89 px. CSS Text 3 4.2 adds letter-spacing to the space (48 px stops, tab 38.89 px). Prediction 32 px stops, sure 2/5.
P6. word-spacing 10px on "a TAB b": stops stay 32 px, tab 24.89 px; CSS Text 3 would add word-spacing (112 px stops). Prediction 32 px, sure 3/5.
P7. "ab<span>c TAB d</span>e" in pre, the span with no sides: the text node gives three fragments (c, tab, d) and the span three rectangles, one per item, the tab from 22.22 to 32. Sure 3/5 for the span's three rectangles (it may report one).
P8. tab-size: 4 on a span holding the tab, the block at 8: "a<span>TAB b</span>": the block's tab-size rules as its font does, the tab from 7.11 to 32 (24.89 px). With the span's, it would end at 16. Prediction 32, sure 3/5.
P9. A justified pre-wrap paragraph with a tab: the tab is an opportunity and takes one share on a line a soft wrap ends, its own advance measured before justification. Sure 2/5.
P10. A justified paragraph with no-break spaces: each no-break space takes one share as a space does. Sure 3/5.
P11. A justified pre-wrap paragraph with double spaces: every preserved space before the line's trailing ones takes one share; the spaces that hang at a soft wrap take none and keep their 4 px each, in a fragment of their own. Sure 3/5.
