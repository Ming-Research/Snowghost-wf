Structural restyle completeness check

Criterion recorded before running the new check:
- Match the whole document before and after each B/X edit, compare winning
  declarations (including custom-rule keys and pseudo-elements) by stable
  NodeId, and count changed surviving elements outside structural_restyle.
- Reject completeness on any missing element in the three pages' block
  scripts, ecma262/html5 blk2, or the focused structure-case fixture.
- Disable feature_children reaches temporarily, rebuild with the mandatory
  locked compiler/shared cache, and require the focused fixture to fail.
- The focused case has independently specified changed counts for position,
  adjacent sibling, descendant-of-sibling, empty, pseudo and custom cases.
  Inserting a paragraph cannot change whether an existing paragraph has an
  h2 preceding sibling for h2 ~ p: that insertion must have zero changes;
  removing the h2 must change the two surviving paragraphs.

Implementation in progress. Cascade fields are private. Without changing
pkg::style, the oracle performs a third full match after the edit using the
original traversal to align rows for rematched. Matching reads ancestry and
sibling positions from the edited DOM. Membership in both fresh traversals
filters detached and inserted subtrees. The fresh post-edit full match also
runs and must succeed. This is deliberately extra work in the oracle, not a
change to the renderer or a performance measurement.

Results: pending.

The new oracle implementation belongs in renderer/oracle/style/structure.wf
and is called by mode structure. Its focused HTML and edit scripts belong
beside the incremental-style scripts and are called by run.sh structure.
Retire them only when this oracle is superseded by equivalent coverage. This
