3. Case page and falsifiers (scripts/block-case.html and .edits, 10 edits)
  seq and par, outputs identical: 8 `inc same`; edits 5 and 6, a
  paragraph inserted into a counted box whose counter a later box shows,
  refused as required. Falsifiers, one change each, built seq:
    F5 the walk's exit state not compared: 5, 6 DIFF (the later box shows
       10 instead of 9)
    F7 the paragraph key's piece comparison and count dropped: 3, 4 DIFF
       (a paragraph split by an inserted block reuses the preparation of
       the whole old paragraph)
    F6 laid not carried into the new context: never DIFF, on the case
       page, on apollo11's 60 block edits or on four small pages written
       to make the parent take its in-place path. Its update counts differ
       (on a box of fixed height: 3 paragraphs broken and 10 entries walked
       against 2 and 6): without laid the parent's in-place path gives up
       and its general path, whose pre-pass writes the child's space again,
       repairs it. Carrying laid is what lets the in-place path run; it is
       a cost, not a correctness, condition, and F6 is no falsifier.
  The first versions of the counter case did not fail F5: the inserted
  paragraph had a ::before (the pseudo-element list check refused it
  first), the counter had no counter-reset (each paragraph opened its own
  instance) and the shown value went from 3 to 4, which the dump's
  rectangles cannot tell apart; the page now resets the counter at 6 so
  the shown value goes from 9 to 10.
