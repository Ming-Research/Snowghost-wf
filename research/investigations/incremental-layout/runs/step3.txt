5. Refusals and limits of this step
   - text_changed refuses (Inconsistent) a node whose new data is empty or
     differs from the old in being only white space, and a node the walk
     met without making a piece (skipped_text) whose new data is not
     empty; the latter is conservative where a table or flex container
     skips white space (white space to other white space there keeps the
     box tree). A node that was in a display:none subtree, or whose text a
     row ignores, changes nothing.
   - Flex, grid, table and replaced contexts on the marked path are laid
     out again in full, after their subtree is returned to the state
     build_boxes leaves (reset_tree), so their intrinsic sizes are
     computed as a full layout computes them.
   - A context's cached intrinsic sizes depend on when they are first
     asked for (docs/todo.md); update reproduces a full layout's order by
     forgetting the marked path's intrinsic sizes before laying anything
     out, returning a marked child to the fresh space before its parent
     asks, and forgetting an unmarked child's when its space changed and
     nobody asked for it yet.
