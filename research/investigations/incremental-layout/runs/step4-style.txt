What was built
  layout_changes maps each table of both results onto classes by value: every
  entry is written as a sequence of words holding all its value (a list a
  group names by the class of that list; a font group's families by the
  names of its range; content lists 0 and 1 and quote lists 0 and 1, which
  are empty lists meaning normal, none, auto and none, by words of their own)
  and the sequences of both runs are interned in one WordTable per table, so
  two entries get one class exactly when their sequences are equal word for
  word. No deep compare is simplified: a list's sequence holds its computed
  tracks with line names, area corners and names, content items with their
  text bytes, counter changes and quote pairs with their text bytes. An element is changed
  when one of the classes of its ten groups differs, an identifier is
  outside its table, or one of its two pseudo-element slots differs. The
  cost is the tables' sizes (tens to hundreds of entries on the three pages)
  plus a dense scan over the elements.
  Two readings of "anything layout reads" narrow the compared groups
  (interface note for the primary agent, see the report): the text group's
  color and the border group's four colours are left out, because pkg::layout
  reads neither (grep of renderer/layout: the only colour read is the
  background group's, inline.wf), and a pseudo-element whose content group is
  normal or none counts as absent, because add_pseudo and add_item_pseudo
  in layout/build.wf return before reading anything else of it.
  `style_oracle delta SCRIPT PAGE UA [SUFFIX=SHEET ...]`: S lines add author
  sheets after the page's own and before the style attributes; C and K add and
  remove a class token (the layout driver's edit_class, copied: the module
  boundary keeps it private); N recomputes with no change; P I prints, after
  edit I's line, `flags I` with the flagged element indices and `dump I` with
  the style dump of the styles after edit I (P 0: the base dump). Each edit
  prints `edit I changed COUNT time_us US`, US being the clock around the
  layout_changes call alone.

Limits
  - The reference is the style dump, so it shares the driver's view of what a
    computed value is, and it prints floats rounded: layout_changes compares
    floats exactly (-0 as 0). No edit here separated the two.
  - The excluded colour columns and the absent-pseudo rule are decisions of
    this step, not of the reference: the reference excludes the same colours
    on purpose, so it cannot confirm that layout reads none of them; the
    grep of renderer/layout (colour reads: inline.wf:780, the background
    group; styles.wf builds fallback groups with colours) is what says so.
  - The three pages' sheets are the saved ones; the only grid, content,
    counter and quote edits are the case page's.
  - Only class edits run: the delta mode does not apply T, D, B or X.
