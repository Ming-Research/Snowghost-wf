Q70's recommendation: (1) a flow context whose marked entry keeps its
outputs is not re-stacked, and one whose entry's height changes by delta
under unchanged margins and floats translates its later entries by delta;
(2) a paragraph's fragments stay relative to its origin; (3) a flex or grid
container updates its marked item in its kept space first and stops when
nothing it reads changed; (4) the html5 contexts laid out again by a later
pass are kept when their final space is unchanged; (5) the --par slowdown on
updates is attributed and recorded. Criterion parts 1, 3 and 4 were run as
in step 3 (runs/step3.txt). Every command ran from the repository root;

What changed (renderer/layout/update.wf unless named)
  - update_flow: when the context's width, flow width, space and definite
    height (or definite_free: no block or child of its flow takes a
    percentage height) are those of its last layout and no marked child is
    positioned, update_in_place runs instead of the pre-pass and the
    stacking pass. It takes at most one marked child and one marked
    paragraph, found by the counts and last index text_changed records
    (marked_children, marked_child, marked_paragraphs, marked_paragraph)
    instead of a scan. The child (an atomic inline, or an in-flow child no
    earlier float reaches: beside) is updated in its kept space; the one
    entry whose layout changed is the paragraph broken again in place at
    its width with no exclusion (refused when a float placed before it
    reaches below its top: Paragraph.beside), or the in-flow child (its
    width, margins and baseline presence unchanged). Height unchanged:
    nothing else moves. Height changed by delta: every block around it
    must grow with it (blocks_grow: auto height, no max-height, length
    vertical paddings, no min-height above its paddings and borders), a
    line box lies in it (no margin collapsing
    through), then translate_after moves every later paragraph, child and
    block by delta (counted loops; paragraphs and blocks found by binary
    search from the entry), translate_fragments moves or grows the
    context's own list (split_fragments' rectangles, now allowed), and
    finish_in_place sets the content height (content_raw + delta), the
    height by used_height, the own fragment, and positions the
    out-of-flow children again. The baseline follows its entry
    (baseline_entry). Anything else falls back to the pre-pass path,
    which keeps what the in-place attempt already did.
  - Fragments (decision 2): paragraph fragments stay relative to the
    paragraph (Paragraph.x, top); place_context adds the position;
    copy_fragments is gone (renderer/layout/flow.wf). Its cost was not
    timed apart: in step 3 it was one of the passes of the 104,321-entry
    walk each html5 edit made (23.3 ms in all, runs/step3.txt), and step
    3b's in-place path never runs it; place_boxes' output is checked by
    the identity runs (the dumps are placed boxes) and by the full dumps
    against main's (section 1).
  - update_container (decision 3) for a marked flex or grid container in
    its kept space with one marked in-flow item: recompute the item's
    intrinsic sizes first when they were known before the edit
    (intrinsic_held, against intrinsic_basis) and require held_min and
    held_max; update the item in its kept space; stop when update_held's
    `still` holds: same outputs (same_outputs), same content height
    (content_raw) and definite_free. Otherwise the container's algorithm
    runs again in update mode, keeping items whose space is unchanged.
    A column flex container never stops (it lays an item out at its
    natural width and again at its line's cross size when it stretches).
    This is the decision with one addition: the decision compares
    border-box size, baseline and margins in the kept space, but a grid
    item stretched to its row, or a flex item stretched to its line, has
    its height forced there, so that comparison cannot see a change of the
    content height the rows pass or the line measures in a height-unknown
    space; content_raw and definite_free make the stop sound for those
    spaces, since grid and row flex containers lay an item out at one
    width in every pass.
  - Decision 4: of the 61 html5 contexts, 60 were absolutely positioned
    children that position_out_with lays out again in the space their
    insets leave (a diagnostic build of step 3's code, made in this step,
    counted the re-laid children by kind; the 61st was not identified).
    settle_child now keeps a positioned child the positioning pass lays
    out again, resolving its margins, and position_out_with keeps it when
    that space is the one it was last laid out in (equivalent_space). Each
    html5 edit now lays out 2 contexts (the timing counts below).
  - The steady test no longer refuses a context whose intrinsic sizes were
    asked for in the update: update_in_place resolves its children's
    margins again first (measured), as the pre-pass path's children pass
    does after intrinsic_flow resolves them against no width.
  - A table or replaced context on the path, laid out in full, has the
    marks below it cleared (clear_tree_marks): ecma262's tables sit in
    `emu-table figure {display:flex}`, whose update reaches the table
    without its flow parent's reset_tree, and a mark left on a cell's
    paragraph hid the next edit of that paragraph from the counts, so it
    was not prepared again (found by ecma262 session-typing: 44 of 88
    edits differed on an intermediate build, every second edit of the
    typing run, and none after this change).

5. Refusals and limits of this step
   - update_in_place refuses: more than one marked child or paragraph; a
     marked child that is a float, out of flow, or beside a float; a
     marked paragraph beside a float (Paragraph.beside); a columned
     context; an entry without lines, or of no height before the edit; a
     height change where a block around the entry cannot grow with it
     (a specified height, a max-height, a percentage vertical padding, a
     min-height above its paddings and borders) or its margins changed. Each refusal falls back to the pre-pass
     path, which re-stacks.
   - update_container stops only for an in-flow item that is a flow
     context or a container that itself stopped; a stretched flex or grid

     runs again (lay_out_child drops them), so run.sh time's counts
     hide that work; the diagnostic build counted it.
   - The delta path's translation of the context's own fragment list is
     checked only by m5 (falsifier 4).
   - When update_container recomputes a nested container's intrinsic
     sizes, its algorithm writes an intrinsic-measuring space into the
     items it measures, and a stop leaves the unmarked ones with
     it; nothing reads a kept child's space before the next pre-pass or
     container pass writes it again, but it is the request-order state
     docs/todo.md's intrinsic-size item describes.
