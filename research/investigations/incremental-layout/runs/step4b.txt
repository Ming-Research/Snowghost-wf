Verdict
  Criteria 3 and 4 hold for style edits. Criterion 2 holds sequentially on
  all three pages and at four workers on ecma262, and fails at four workers
  on html5 (1.37) and apollo11 (2.43), the two pages where a root font
  change reaches nearly every paragraph: on html5 the update prepares all
  60,868 paragraphs and lays out all 13,843 contexts, the same work as the
  full layout, in 351 ms against the full path's 249 ms (edit 1, run 1),
  while sequentially the two take 792 and 802 ms. The update path's walk
  over a fully marked tree is less parallel than build_layout and lay_out.
  Separately, an update that does no layout work costs 5 to 9 times as much
  at four workers as sequentially (colour edits: 27.6 against 3.8 ms on
  html5), the tree-wide validation and marking walk of styles_changed.
  Neither is attributed yet; both are recorded in docs/todo.md.

  is called from: a Whitefoot scheduling effect, not one of the update's
