Node: pipeline

Decision: Every renderer stage keeps only the data dependencies of its algorithm and writes all its other work in forms the Whitefoot compiler proves independent (counted loops whose iterations write disjoint places, recursions over disjoint halves, independent statements), taking extra or speculative work where it shortens the critical path, such as breaking every paragraph into lines before floats are placed and breaking again only the paragraphs a float narrows, and leaves which independent work runs in parallel, and at what grain, to the compiler and its runtime, because a stage's time on P cores is about its work divided by P plus its critical path, so its parallelism is bounded by its chains of true dependencies rather than by a chosen unit, and Whitefoot proves independence at every grain, instead of a fixed unit of parallel work. This retires the node's decision beginning "The independent formatting context, meaning a block formatting context root"; whether that context remains the unit of storage, invalidation or caching is an open question of the concurrency investigation, not a decision.

Rejected:
- The independent formatting context as the unit of parallel work: rejected because on each of the three measured real pages one block formatting context holds 85 to 95 percent of the text, so layout split only between contexts ran at 0.84 to 0.96 times the sequential build at four workers ([layout measurement](../../research/investigations/concurrency/DESIGN.md#layout-measurement)).
- Breaking a context's paragraphs in parallel only when no float is among its boxes: rejected because the dominant context holds a float on two of the three pages, where the rule gained nothing, while breaking lines needs no float placement until the lines beside a float.
